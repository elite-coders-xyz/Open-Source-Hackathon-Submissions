## Project Details

### Project Title
**Sanjeevani – AI-Powered Healthcare Billing Transparency Platform**

### Project Description

Sanjeevani is an AI-powered healthcare billing transparency platform built to combat medical billing fraud and overcharging in India.

The platform automatically scans hospital bills using OCR, extracts medical procedures and charges, cross-references them against **1,995 official CGHS government healthcare rates**, and identifies potential overcharges in real time.

Using a dual-engine OCR pipeline (EasyOCR + Tesseract), intelligent document parsing, fuzzy matching, and AI-powered auditing, Sanjeevani helps patients understand medical expenses, verify billing accuracy, and make informed financial decisions.

---

### Key Features

- **AI-powered hospital bill auditing** - automated, line-by-line analysis of uploaded bills
- **Dual-engine OCR** (EasyOCR + Tesseract) for robust extraction across document types
- **Automated overcharge detection** - flags discrepancies against government rate benchmarks
- **Cross-verification against 1,995 CGHS procedures** using fuzzy matching
- **Real-time analytics dashboard** - visual breakdown of charges and savings identified
- **Jan Aushadhi medicine locator** - connects patients to affordable generic alternatives
- **Government healthcare scheme discovery** - eligibility lookup for patients
- **Secure authentication and document management** via JWT
- **OpenAPI-documented backend APIs** for full transparency and extensibility

---

### Impact

Medical billing fraud and inflated healthcare costs affect millions of patients across India. Sanjeevani promotes **transparency, accountability, and accessibility** by giving every patient a simple way to audit hospital bills, identify unfair charges, and exercise their right to accurate billing - without needing legal or medical expertise.
