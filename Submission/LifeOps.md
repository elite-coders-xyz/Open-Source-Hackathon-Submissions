## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
LifeOps

**Project Description:**  
*The AI that handles the annoying decisions you make every day.*  

LifeOps is an open everyday life admin workspace. It gives visitors one calm place for food decisions, spending analysis, difficult message drafting, and an Adulting Score that turns small actions into useful patterns.

### ✨ Key Features:
* **Zero-Friction Workspace:** Fully open with no login wall.
* **Food Decider:** AI-powered food suggestions based on budget, diet, cuisine, mood, time, and health preferences, with deterministic fallbacks and history tracking.
* **Money Analyzer:** Effortless spending analysis with CSV imports (up to 500 txns), merchant categorization, manual expense CRUD, filters, CSV exports, analytics charts, and practical insights.
* **Message Generator:** Draft difficult messages by specifying the situation, tone, recipient, and context. Includes editable variants, copy, regenerate, and text download.
* **Adulting Score Engine:** Turns small actions into useful patterns by analyzing budget awareness, tracking consistency, spending spread, food variety, and activity breakdowns.

**Tech Stack Used:**  
* **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn-style UI primitives, Recharts, Framer Motion
* **AI Integration:** OpenAI & Gemini REST APIs (with server-only secrets & deterministic fallbacks)
* **Database & ORM:** Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL-ready schema
* **State & Validation:** Zod, React Hook Form
