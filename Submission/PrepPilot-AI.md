## Project Details

**Project Title:** PrepPilot AI: AI-Powered Interview Preparation Platform

**Project Description:** PrepPilot AI is a modern, full-stack interview preparation platform that helps students and job seekers improve their interview performance through AI-driven resume analysis, mock interviews, personalized feedback, and learning roadmaps. 

The platform enables users to upload resumes, receive AI-generated insights, participate in realistic HR and technical mock interviews, answer questions through voice interaction, and receive detailed performance evaluations covering confidence, communication, relevance, grammar, and technical accuracy. Users can track progress over time through a comprehensive analytics dashboard.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, pgvector, and Gemini/OpenAI integrations, PrepPilot AI demonstrates a production-ready application that addresses one of the most critical challenges faced by students and professionals: effective interview preparation.

**Tech Stack Used:** Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma ORM, Neon PostgreSQL, pgvector, Google Gemini API, Nodemailer SMTP
