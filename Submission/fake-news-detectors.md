## Project Details

**Project Title:**
AI Truth Engine – Fake News Detection System

**Project Description:**
AI Truth Engine is an advanced AI-powered Fake News Detection platform that helps users verify the authenticity of news articles using Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing techniques. The platform supports multilingual news analysis, user authentication, profile management, analysis history tracking, and AI-based credibility scoring to help reduce the spread of misinformation.
