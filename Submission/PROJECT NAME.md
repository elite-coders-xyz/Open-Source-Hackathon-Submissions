## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
ShieldHer – AI Powered Women Safety System

**Project Description:**  

ShieldHer is an AI-powered women safety solution that provides instant emergency assistance through voice activation and hardware-based triggers. The system is designed to help women quickly alert their trusted contacts and share their real-time location during emergencies.

When activated through a voice command or emergency button, ShieldHer automatically:

-Sends emergency SMS alerts to saved contacts
-Initiates emergency phone calls
-Shares live GPS location
-Activates a multi-layer hardware safety mechanism

The project combines Artificial Intelligence, IoT, and Full Stack Web Development to create a real-time smart safety ecosystem. By reducing response time during critical situations, ShieldHer aims to improve personal safety and provide immediate access to help.
