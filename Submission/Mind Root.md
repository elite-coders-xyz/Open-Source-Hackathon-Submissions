## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
Mind Root 

**Project Description:**  
MindRoot is a personal growth and mental wellness platform that helps users develop self-awareness, build positive habits, and maintain emotional well-being through a single, unified experience. Many people use separate applications for journaling, habit tracking, mood monitoring, and mindfulness, making it difficult to gain a holistic view of their personal growth. MindRoot solves this problem by bringing these essential wellness tools together in one intuitive platform.

Users can track their daily moods, maintain reflective journal entries, manage habits, practice guided breathing exercises, and view analytics that highlight their progress over time. By combining productivity with mental wellness, MindRoot encourages consistent self-reflection and healthy routines. The project matters because it promotes emotional awareness, mindfulness, and sustainable self-improvement, helping individuals better understand themselves and make meaningful progress toward their personal goals.


**Tech Stack Used:**  
React (v19), Vite (v6), TailwindCSS (v3) + PostCSS + Autoprefixer, Framer Motion 
Analytics & Visualization: Recharts (for interactive mood lines, distribution pies, and consistency bars)
Icons: Lucide React (for premium minimalist iconography)
