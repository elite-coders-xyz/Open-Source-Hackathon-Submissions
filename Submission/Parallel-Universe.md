## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
Parallel Universe

**Project Description:**  
Parallel Universe is an AI-powered decision intelligence platform that simulates alternate future pathways based on user choices. Leveraging Google's Gemini AI, the platform analyzes decisions, compares potential outcomes, and generates personalized insights to help users make informed academic, career, and life decisions. By visualizing multiple future scenarios, it enables users to evaluate opportunities, risks, and long-term impacts before making critical decisions.
