## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
Rhythma

**Project Description:**  
Rhythma is an AI-powered women’s health companion designed specifically for women across India. While menstrual health forms a core part of the platform, Rhythma takes a broader view of well-being by combining cycle tracking, symptoms, sleep, stress, lifestyle factors, and personalized health insights. The project aims to improve menstrual health awareness and make wellness support more accessible through technology.

**Tech Stack Used:**  
Flutter, FastAPI, Firebase, Hive, Gemini API, Python
