## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
Prompt Master

**Project Description:**  
PromptMaster V2 is an AI-powered prompt engineering platform that transforms raw ideas into structured, production-ready prompts through a three-stage workflow: Plan, Build, and Optimize. Instead of spending time manually crafting complex prompts for coding, product development, or AI workflows, users can instantly generate high-quality prompts tailored for execution. The platform provides real-time AI streaming, prompt history, team collaboration, and workspace management, making prompt engineering faster, more consistent, and scalable. By simplifying the process of creating effective prompts, PromptMaster V2 helps developers, founders, freelancers, and AI enthusiasts turn ideas into actionable results with greater speed and accuracy.


**Tech Stack Used:**  
Frontend: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript

Backend: Node.js, Express.js

Authentication: Clerk

Database: PostgreSQL (Supabase)

Caching & Rate Limiting: Upstash Redis

AI Model: Groq (LLaMA 3.3 70B)

Payments: Stripe

Email Services: Resend

Security: Helmet.js, CORS

Real-Time Streaming: Server-Sent Events (SSE)
