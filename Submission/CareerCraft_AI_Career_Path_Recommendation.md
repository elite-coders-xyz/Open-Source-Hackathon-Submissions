## Project Details

**Project Title:** CareerCraft: AI-Powered Career Path Recommendation & Smart Recruitment Platform

**Project Description:** CareerCraft is a full-stack AI-powered "Super App" that bridges the gap between job seekers and recruiters. It helps students, graduates, and career switchers explore tech roles through AI-driven personalized roadmaps, gamified learning, and a suite of intelligent career tools. Key features include an AI chatbot powered by Google Gemini for career assessments, interactive step-by-step roadmaps (Data Science, AI Engineering, Full Stack, Cybersecurity) with real-time progress tracking, a gamified dashboard with points/streaks/badges, an AI Resume Builder with ATS-friendly PDF export, a 1-click Portfolio Generator, and a B2B HR portal for smart candidate matchmaking. The platform solves the real-world problem of career confusion for tech aspirants while simultaneously providing recruiters a data-rich pipeline of verified, skilled candidates.

**Tech Stack Used:** React 19, Vite 7, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB (Mongoose), Google Gemini AI API, JWT Authentication, bcryptjs, Nodemailer, Helmet, Express-Rate-Limit, Vanilla CSS (Glassmorphism & Neon UI), Vercel (Frontend), Render (Backend)
