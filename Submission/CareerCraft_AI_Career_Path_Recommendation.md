## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
CareerCraft: AI-Powered Career Path Recommendation & Smart Recruitment Platform

**Project Description:**  
CareerCraft is a full-stack AI-powered Super App that bridges the gap between job seekers and recruiters. Features include AI chatbot powered by Google Gemini for career assessments, interactive roadmaps for Data Science, AI Engineering, Full Stack and Cybersecurity with real-time progress tracking, gamified dashboard with points/streaks/badges, AI Resume Builder with ATS PDF export, 1-click Portfolio Generator, and a B2B HR portal for smart candidate matchmaking.

**Tech Stack Used:**  
React 19, Vite 7, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB (Mongoose), Google Gemini AI API, JWT Authentication, bcryptjs, Nodemailer, Helmet, Express-Rate-Limit, Vanilla CSS, Vercel (Frontend), Render (Backend)
