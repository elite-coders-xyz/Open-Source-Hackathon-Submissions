## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
AEGESIS – AI-Powered Women Safety & Online Threat Intelligence Platform


**Project Description:**  
AEGESIS is an AI-powered women safety and online threat intelligence platform designed to proactively identify, prevent, and respond to both digital and real-world safety threats.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, real-time location tracking, emergency response systems, digital identity protection, and secure evidence management into a single privacy-focused ecosystem.

Unlike traditional safety applications that react only after emergencies occur, AEGESIS predicts risks, protects users in real time, automates emergency response, and securely preserves evidence for future action.

Key capabilities include:
- AI Message Analyzer
- Threat Detection
- Grooming & Manipulation Detection
- Live Location Tracking
- Safe Route Suggestions
- Panic Button
- Trusted Contact Alerts
- Smart Evidence Vault
- Privacy Controls
- Emotional AI Support
