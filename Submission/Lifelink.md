## Project Details

**Project Title:**  
 Life link - smart vaccine monitoring system

**Project Description:**  
<!-- Explain what your project does, what problem it solves, and why it matters -->
What My Project Does

LifeLink is an AI and Blockchain-powered vaccine cold chain monitoring system that ensures vaccines remain safe and effective during transportation and storage. The system uses an ESP32 microcontroller and DHT22 sensor to continuously monitor temperature and humidity in real time.

When unsafe temperature conditions are detected, the device immediately activates local alerts through LEDs and a buzzer. Sensor data is sent to a Flask-based backend where AI logic analyzes thermal exposure and estimates vaccine potency loss. Every record is secured using SHA-256 blockchain-style hashing to create a tamper-resistant history of environmental conditions.

Problem It Solves

Vaccines are highly sensitive biological products that must be maintained within a specific temperature range during transportation and storage. Temperature excursions can reduce vaccine effectiveness, resulting in wasted doses, financial losses, and potential public health risks.

Traditional monitoring systems often provide limited traceability and can be vulnerable to data manipulation. In many regions, especially during long-distance transportation, it is difficult to verify whether vaccines have remained within safe conditions throughout the journey.

LifeLink addresses these challenges by providing:

- Real-time temperature monitoring
- Instant local alerts for temperature breaches
- AI-based vaccine potency estimation
- Secure and tamper-resistant data logging
- End-to-end visibility of vaccine handling conditions

Why It Matters

Maintaining the vaccine cold chain is critical for global healthcare and immunization programs. A single temperature breach can compromise thousands of vaccine doses, affecting patient safety and increasing healthcare costs.

LifeLink combines IoT, Artificial Intelligence, and Blockchain technologies to create a smarter and more trustworthy cold chain system. By helping healthcare providers monitor vaccine conditions, predict potency degradation, and ensure data integrity, the system improves transparency, reduces wastage, and supports safer vaccine distribution.

This project demonstrates how emerging technologies can be integrated to solve real-world healthcare logistics challenges and strengthen public health infrastructure.
